# Receiving non-conformance procedure

Written for Officine Meccaniche Riviera. This is not the text of any published standard.

1. A batch is released only when every measurement is inside the tolerance of the drawing revision named on the purchase order, the documents agree, and the gauge was in date.
2. A failed measurement quarantines the batch. It does not go onto the line.
3. If the gauge was out of date, or two documents name different heat numbers, the batch is held until a person resolves it. Those measurements are not used.
4. A person approves every claim before it is sent, and every release under concession.
