# Quality agreement — Officine Bianchi

Plant procedure for Officine Meccaniche Riviera. Written for this demo.

## Q-4 Dimensional defects

Dimensional nonconformities against the drawing revision named on the purchase order are the supplier's responsibility.

## Q-9 Revision named on the order

The supplier is only responsible for the drawing revision written on the purchase order. A part that meets that revision is not a supplier defect, even if it fails a later revision.
