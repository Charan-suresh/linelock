# Quality agreement — Müller Stahl GmbH

Plant procedure for Officine Meccaniche Riviera. Written for this demo.

## Q-4 Dimensional defects

Dimensional nonconformities against the drawing revision named on the purchase order are the supplier's responsibility. The supplier bears the cost of return or replacement of the affected quantity.

## Q-5 Material grade

A material grade that differs from the grade named on the drawing is the supplier's responsibility. The affected quantity is not machined.

## Q-7 Exclusions

Marks that do not affect a functional surface are excluded from supplier liability. The plant decides whether to use the parts under concession.
