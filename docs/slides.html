1 · Problem

Stop a bad forging before it is machined into a brake bracket.

Giulia at receiving has a delivery note, a material certificate, the supplier's inspection report, and the shop's own measurement. The check is often late.

No measured number on this slide.

2 · Product

Release, quarantine, or hold. Then a person decides.

Code checks sizes, identities, and the gauge date. The model maps the supplier's feature names, and on a failure it quotes the contract and drafts the claim. The model cannot release the batch.

No measured number on this slide.

5 · What we do not claim

Incoming inspection already exists.

LineLock does not replace a quality system. The shop, the contracts, and the packets are written for this demo. Extraction in this build is local. The model is the local stand-in until it is pointed at AMD.

The four decisions: the revision on the purchase order, an expired gauge, a claim only when a sentence covers the defect, and a small-shop upload gate.

Source: linelock-plan.md section 11. No measured number.
